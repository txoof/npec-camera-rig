The places are where the trays stand in the latest photo, in rows and columns, empty spots too. Randomize gives every tray a random place and says which way round it lies: A1 at SW or NE for a 4 x 5 tray, turn round or keep as is for a 2 x 2 tray. A row of plants that was on the table edge (north, south, west or east) is not put on the edge again.

Move the trays, then press Check layout. The rig takes a photo and marks each tray that is not where the layout says, with an arrow to its place or a turning arrow.
