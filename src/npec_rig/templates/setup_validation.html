What validation shows, and how to do a round

Calibration alone cannot show how accurate area measurements are in each tray slot, because it is checked against the same tags it was fitted to. Validation moves the tag sheets to different trays and new positions, then measures every tag again. When a slot gives similar results across several rounds, its error belongs to that slot, not to a particular sheet, and the slot's ±% figure is what a new object measured there should expect.

Each round: leave the trays where they are. Put each sheet on a different tray, 2 to 5 cm from where a sheet lay before, flat and fully on the tray, one sheet per tray. Step out of view, close the chamber door, tick "The chamber door is closed", then press Capture round. 3 counted rounds complete a validation; more rounds only add tags.

Close the door before every round: an open door changes the air and light in the chamber, and readings taken with people working and the door open changed more than with the chamber closed. Spread the rounds over at least 20 minutes, first to last: readings rise and fall on a cycle of about 19 minutes, and rounds taken close together all see one point of it. A validation with rounds closer together still counts, but says so.
