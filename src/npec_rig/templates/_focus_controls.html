What do these controls do? {% if show_square_mode %}

Focus mode: click the preview to place the focus ROI; the magnified crop updates to match. Square mode: drag the guide-line tabs at the frame's edge to line them up with a real edge, checking by eye that the rig sits square: a straightening aid, not a saved reading.

Focus peaking highlights high-contrast edges in the crop above in magenta. Denser highlighting means sharper focus; use it alongside the sharpness number, not instead of it.

Lock focus moves the lens to the position you set, always the same way, and waits until the camera reports it there; calibration and validation then hold to that position. While focus is locked the lens controls and Autofocus are greyed out; press Unlock focus to change it.
