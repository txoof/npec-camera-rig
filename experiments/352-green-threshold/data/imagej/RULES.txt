#352 Rulebook for the ImageJ leaf outlines
Goal: the same plant outlined twice by these rules gives the same area. One pixel is about
0.2 mm, so a leaf edge is blurred over 3 to 4 pixels. Most of the decisions below are about that blur.

Before you start
R1. Do not look at any rig outline (yellow or cyan pictures) before or during the outlining.
R2. Same screen, same room light, every pass. Do not change brightness or contrast in ImageJ.
R3. Zoom 800% for every crop (Image > Zoom > Set, 800).
R4. Polygon tool for every crop, not freehand. Place a point at least every 2 pixels along curves.

What is the plant
R5. Only the plant whose centre is nearest the crop centre. Leave out neighbours, moss and specks.
R6. Count everything green or yellow-green that is attached to it: true leaves, cotyledons, stalks.
R7. Overlapping leaves: outline the outside of the group once. The rig sees from above, so
    overlap counts once.
R8. Soil seen between leaves: leave it out (Alt + polygon subtracts a hole) when the gap is at
    least 2 pixels wide. Narrower gaps are part of the plant.
R9. Dark shadow next to a leaf is soil, unless you can see leaf colour or texture in it.
R10. Pale, yellow or brown leaf parts that are still part of a leaf count as plant.

Where the edge is
R11. Trace through the middle of the blur: the pixels halfway between leaf colour and soil colour.
     Not the outer edge of the blur, not the inner edge.
R12. If a part is doubtful (more than about 4 pixels across and you are not sure it is plant),
     include it if more than half of it looks like leaf, and write the file name and a few words
     in notes.txt.

Repeat, to measure your own spread
R13. Do two passes. Pass 1 in file order. Pass 2 on another day, in reverse order, without opening
     pass 1. Save results_pass1.csv / roi_pass1.zip and results_pass2.csv / roi_pass2.zip.
R14. Do each pass in one sitting. Write the date, start and end time in notes.txt.
R15. Do not correct pass 1 after seeing pass 2. A difference between passes is a result.

What this reference does not cover
- Leaves hidden under other leaves, and leaf height (the rig measures area seen from above).
- Other people: one person's outlines show one person's judgement.
